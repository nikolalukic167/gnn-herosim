# scale_160_v1 — does a GNN beat CD at 160 clients × 24 servers?

**Status:** `REGISTERED` (2026-10-10). No data yet. The go/no-go screen is running. Every bar below was signed before
its data.

**Why.** At 40c × 6s the placement problem is close to trivial:
- median candidates per task is 2–3, and 11–37 % of slots have one candidate;
- median plans per batch are 8 / 24 / 45;
- a random plan is optimal 11–24 % of the time;
- only 20 of the 40 clients send.

`r1_attribution_v1` found that every learned arm loses to CD and that MP ties its twin there. The scale feasibility
report (S5, probe job 855065, `rp/scale-probe` 38aa87ab) found that at 160c × 24s with p = 0.6, every task has a full
top-5 slate. There, 69 % of batches carry more than 1 % joint (non-pointwise) headroom, with median regret about 15 %,
against 36 % and 0 % at 40 × 6 (one seed, about 50 batches). Exact labels on the declared top-5 slate cost about
33 node-h.

## Design (fixed now)

- **Environment.**
  - Physics: R1.1 + the T-fix, as in `r1_attribution_v1`.
  - Topology: 160 clients × 24 servers, connectivity p = 0.6, generated by `src/generate_infrastructure.py`.
  - Workload: the WF1 windows spread over the clients as in the probe (`scale_probe_remap_clients.py`, disclosed).
  - Rungs: moderate and heavy, re-derived at this size by the `load_recalibration_v1` protocol (CD effective share in
    the moderate and heavy bands).
- **Label.** Exact brute force over the declared top-5 slate, sub-batched to at most 4 tasks above 100k plans, as in
  node 5. The replay is **F** (`58364ab0`), which the variant decision made the replay for future builds.
- **Corpus.** About 2,500 batches from about 50 train topologies and 8 held-out topologies (validation and selection).
- **Arms.** CD (the bar, full candidate set) against `gnn_eng` and `gnn_eng_physmp`.
  - Each arm gets 6 configs × 3 seeds, is selected on validation only, and has the 100-epoch cap.
  - No raw, set-transformer or MLP arms (user scope).
  - The MP-off twin (`twin_eng`) is trained on the same corpus only if a GNN wins. Before that, no sentence may claim
    "message passing helps".
- **Gate.** 12 fresh test topologies (minted, disjoint from train and held-out) × 2 rungs × 4 windows × seeds 1, 2.
  - Statistic: paired % per topology (median over windows and seeds), then the median over topologies, with an exact
    Wilcoxon.
- **Primary family.** {gnn_eng vs CD, physmp vs CD} × {moderate, heavy}, Holm over all 4.
- **Win.** A GNN arm is at median ≤ −5 % with Holm p < 0.05 at both rungs, and no rung has CD Holm-confirmed faster.
  Anything less is reported by label (DIRECTION-ONLY / NOT-SEPARATED / CD-FASTER).
- **Secondary, descriptive.** Self-predict, CD←GNN, decision cost, and the spin counters (deferrals,
  reachability failures, averageWaitTime).

## Go/no-go before any build (signed 2026-10-10 01:30)

- (a) Probe at 160 × 24 p = 0.6 on 4 fresh topology seeds, 12 batches × 2 rungs × 2 windows. **GO** if at least 3 of 4
  seeds have more than 50 % of batches above 1 % pointwise regret, and 0 captures hang.
- (b) A 40-state I11 replay check at this size: median ≤ 1 %, p95 ≤ 1 %, max ≤ 5 %.
- (c) A 2-step CD bisection confirms the heavy band (CD effective share 0.4–0.5) is reachable with no guard failure.
- All three must pass before the build. Backup if the label-gap share binds: 320c × 48s at p = 0.3.
