The named, gnn-side failures are (i) a co-location loss in one topology and window (30292 g0, seeds 2-3) and (ii) putting 41 % of a batch's transfers onto one WiFi downlink (30297 g1 seed 3). Together they are 3 of the 72 heavy gnn cells, in two topologies; neither is shown to be systematic.
