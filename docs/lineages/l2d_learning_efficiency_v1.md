# l2d_learning_efficiency_v1 — size-specific learning efficiency

**Status:** `CLOSED` (2026-09-23) — no two-size GNN learning-efficiency win over both pointwise controls at either tested ratio. Registered 2026-09-23; the 2,000-update midpoint was registered after the 3× failure and read on a fresh split.

**Outcome.** The frozen 1,000-vs-3,000-update gate passed only one of four required contrasts. The fresh-split 2,000-vs-3,000 follow-up passed three of four: the GNN beat typed MLP at both sizes and MP-OFF at 6×6, but its 10×10 gain over MP-OFF was uncertain (+1.12%, bootstrap 95% CI −2.70 to +4.45). Thus these runs do not establish a graph-specific sample-efficiency advantage across both sizes. They do show a narrower advantage over the typed MLP with 33% fewer generated training instances at the midpoint. The hand FDD/MWKR rule still has lower makespan than the learned arms on the same fresh instances. This closes these two budgets, rates, features, and seeds, not all possible data-efficiency settings.

**Question.** Does the L2D GNN need less training experience than matched pointwise controls to reach a given quality when every model is trained at the job-shop size it serves? The [parent study](literature_reeval_v1.md) measured a within-size GNN advantage after longer training; [zero-shot size transfer](l2d_size_transfer_v1.md) failed. Neither result establishes learning efficiency.

## Frozen gate

- **Arms and sizes:** Train `gnn`, `mpoff`, and typed receptive-field `mlp_t1` separately at 6×6 and 10×10, using the upstream L2D environment and the parent's architecture and feature contracts. Eight paired training seeds (0–7) per arm and size give 48 runs. Seed `s` uses the parent's `torch_seed=600+s` and `np_seed_train=1000+s`.
- **Training budget:** Each run uses 3,000 PPO updates with four generated training instances per update. Save the *fixed-update* weights and `.contract.json` sidecar at updates 1,000 and 3,000. Neither checkpoint is selected by validation performance. Log every run to Weights & Biases. The cluster launch script is [`l2d_learning_efficiency_v1.sbatch`](../../scripts_cosim/datalab/l2d_learning_efficiency_v1.sbatch).
- **Learning rates:** Use the rates selected in the earlier within-size study: `5e-4` for `gnn` and `mpoff` at both sizes, `5e-4` for `mlp_t1` at 6×6, and `1e-4` for `mlp_t1` at 10×10. All other PPO settings follow the parent study. These rates were selected using earlier validation data, before the fresh test set below.
- **Held-out evaluation:** Evaluate both fixed checkpoints for every run on the same 100 fresh, deterministic instances per size generated with NumPy seed 302. Use greedy decoding in the upstream L2D environment and record every instance makespan, its mean, checkpoint and sidecar hashes, and the dataset hash. The seed-302 instances must not be used for learning-rate choice, checkpoint selection, or protocol changes.
- **Unit and statistic:** Pair arms by training seed within each size. For a GNN checkpoint `g` and control checkpoint `c`, define seed-level improvement as `100 × (mean_makespan(c) − mean_makespan(g)) / mean_makespan(c)`; positive favors the GNN. Report all eight paired improvements, their mean, and a two-sided 95% bootstrap confidence interval for that mean by resampling the eight paired seeds, with a fixed bootstrap RNG seed. The 100 shared instances are evaluation cases, not 100 independent training replicates.
- **Primary learning-efficiency contrast:** Compare `gnn` at update 1,000 against *each* control at update 3,000, separately at 6×6 and 10×10. Claim **positive GNN learning efficiency** only if the mean improvement is positive and the bootstrap 95% interval's lower bound exceeds zero for **both controls at both sizes**. Otherwise the two-size claim fails; report which contrast did not pass.
- **Secondary contrasts:** Compare `gnn` with each control at the same budget, at updates 1,000 and 3,000. Report these with confidence intervals, without using them to replace or relax the primary rule.

The previously opened training and validation curves are exploratory context. The fixed-update checkpoints and seed-302 held-out outcomes are required for this gate; validation curves or best-validation checkpoints cannot answer its primary contrast. A pass would establish a result for these two sizes, training budgets, rates, and L2D implementation, not universal sample efficiency. A failure would leave open other budgets and training recipes.

## Record

- **2026-09-23 — midpoint follow-up FAIL (SLURM 803126).** All 48 seed-303 evaluations completed; the 16 new GNN checkpoints and all 32 reused control checkpoints matched their recorded hashes. At 6×6, GNN at 2,000 updates improved on MP-OFF at 3,000 by **4.35%** (paired-seed bootstrap 95% CI **0.60 to 7.90**, 6/8 positive) and on typed MLP by **12.05%** (**7.36 to 16.28**, 8/8). At 10×10, the corresponding improvements were **1.12%** versus MP-OFF (**−2.70 to 4.45**, 6/8) and **6.03%** versus typed MLP (**0.07 to 11.82**, 7/8). Three of four required lower bounds were positive; the two-size, both-controls claim fails. On the same seed-303 instances, FDD/MWKR averaged 559.77 at 6×6 and 957.62 at 10×10 versus GNN means of 586.08 and 1008.44. These results use 8,000 versus 12,000 generated training instances; inference actions per instance differ by size, and no CPU-time-efficiency claim follows. This post-hoc budget was tested on new seed-303 instances, but uses training seeds and learning rates already inspected in the parent study. Evidence: [follow-up analysis](l2d_learning_efficiency_v1/followup_analysis_2026-09-23.json), [sealed follow-up results and new checkpoints](l2d_learning_efficiency_v1/sealed_followup_2026-09-23.tar.gz), [6×6 hand-rule results](l2d_learning_efficiency_v1/pdr_seed303_6x6.json), [10×10 hand-rule results](l2d_learning_efficiency_v1/pdr_seed303_10x10.json), and [analyzer](../../scripts_reeval/l2d/analyze_learning_efficiency_followup.py).
- **2026-09-23 — frozen 3× gate FAIL (SLURM 803055).** All 48 runs completed, and all 96 milestone checkpoints and sidecars matched the hashes recorded by the evaluator. The 6×6 and 10×10 test sets each had one common hash across arms. At 6×6, GNN at 1,000 updates improved on MP-OFF at 3,000 by a paired-seed mean **4.36%** (bootstrap 95% CI **−3.02 to 9.80**, 7/8 positive) and on typed MLP by **12.70%** (**5.59 to 17.89**, 7/8). At 10×10, the corresponding improvements were **−0.90%** versus MP-OFF (**−9.55 to 5.94**, 6/8) and **3.41%** versus typed MLP (**−6.30 to 11.94**, 5/8). Only one of four required contrasts passed, so the two-size 3× learning-efficiency claim fails. This is a failed registered claim, not evidence that message passing never helps: at equal 3,000-update budgets, GNN beat MP-OFF by **4.02%** (CI **−0.84 to 7.85**) at 6×6 and **3.91%** (**1.37 to 6.19**) at 10×10; it beat typed MLP by **12.37%** (**7.21 to 16.67**) and **8.06%** (**2.51 to 13.42**) respectively. Eight reused training seeds give wide intervals; the fresh test instances do not make those seeds independent of earlier learning-rate selection. Evidence: [analysis](l2d_learning_efficiency_v1/analysis_2026-09-23.json), [sealed results and fixed checkpoints](l2d_learning_efficiency_v1/sealed_gate_2026-09-23.tar.gz), and [analyzer](../../scripts_reeval/l2d/analyze_fixed_budget.py).
- **2026-09-23 — follow-up registered after reading the failed gate, before seed-303 evaluation.** The natural midpoint asks whether GNN at **2,000 updates** (8,000 generated training instances) beats each existing pointwise control at **3,000 updates** (12,000 instances). Train only 16 new GNN runs (the same eight seeds and selected rates at both sizes), save fixed-update weights at 2,000, and evaluate those plus the existing 32 control checkpoints on 100 fresh, shared NumPy-seed-303 instances per size. Use the same paired-seed improvement, fixed-seed 100,000-resample bootstrap 95% intervals, and all-four-positive-lower-bounds pass rule. This is a **post-hoc budget tweak**; its independent test split protects that read from direct seed-302 test-set tuning, but it cannot retroactively pass the 3× gate. Launch: [follow-up sbatch](../../scripts_cosim/datalab/l2d_learning_efficiency_v1_followup.sbatch). At registration, no seed-303 result had been read.
- **2026-09-23 — registered.** The arms, rates, two budgets, fresh evaluation split, paired unit, and all-four-contrasts pass rule were written before any seed-302 result was opened. Existing validation curves and the parent study informed the design; they are not the held-out result.
