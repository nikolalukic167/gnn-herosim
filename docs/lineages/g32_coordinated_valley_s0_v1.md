**Outcome.** Material two-task valleys exist, but only 18 of 3,156 joint candidates qualify, and their bounded oracle improves the source by **1.03%** at the median topology, far below the signed 8% bar. **Question.** Under [core-only peer prefetch](g32_core_prefetch_s0_v1.md), can two task reassignments improve a 32-task placement when either reassignment alone worsens its exact RTT, and does that improvement survive an equally budgeted route-aware hand search? The parent local-move target is closed because its strong hand control captured 72–100% of bounded oracle gain. Its exploratory second-round search gained 8.22% beyond the first oracle but gave the simple hand proxy 57.2% capture. This successor tests a different, genuinely coordinated decision rather than extending that opened result into a training claim.

Only **18/3,156 (0.57%)** candidates are material valleys, spread over **15/48** groups. The best individual witness improves a group by 5.88% while each constituent single worsens it by 1.17% and 5.84%; that establishes the mechanism can occur but not sufficient aggregate value. The median material-valley oracle gain is **1.03%**, failing the 8% bar. Material-valley prevalence reaches 25% of groups on only **2/4** topologies, failing the signed 3/4 bar. All-pair oracle gain has median **16.99%**, but the stronger equal-budget route-aware control captures at least **91%** on every held-out topology, failing the under-half search-hardness bar. The 451-feature control includes local candidate state, platform loads, peer 1/2/4-hop summaries, per-core-link route loads and pair-specific physical proxies. It gets no exact constituent-single RTT at inference. These are bounded-pool results, not global optimality claims.

Core wait shares are **18.06%, 16.81%, 13.46%, 13.38%** of RTT for 9823 hand/MP-OFF and 9824 hand/MP-OFF. This confirms the physics, not a valley-policy gain. No GPU was allocated.

## 2026-09-23 — signed CPU protocol before pair scores

Use the isolated `/tmp/herosim_peer_coreonly` finite core-only prefetch copy; do not edit fingerprinted repository physics. Frozen inputs are the captured first 16 groups on training topologies 9801, 9803, 9804 and 9806, the four-type workload, and the parent's exact baseline scores. Groups 0–3 of each topology were opened by exploratory pair and second-round probes, so this qualification scores **groups 4–15 only**. These groups and topologies are still development data because the parent local-move oracle and controls opened them; do not call them sealed validation.

For each of 48 groups, choose the lower exact-RTT hand or MP-OFF complete plan as source using only the parent's two baseline evaluations. Generate up to 128 distinct pair plans deterministically: sort peer edges by decreasing payload and task indices; for each edge enumerate both tasks' valid target placements on the same node, retaining only plans that change both tasks, in candidate order. This pool is fixed before its RTT labels. Exact-score the two constituent single reassignments and their joint plan from the same source snapshot, memoizing duplicate plans. A **strict valley** has both singles worse than source and the joint better, each by more than 1e-8 seconds. A **material valley** requires each single to worsen source RTT by at least 0.5% and the joint to improve it by at least 2%. Report pool coverage, exact evaluation count, per-topology group prevalence, and bounded all-pair, strict-valley and material-valley oracles. None is a global optimum.

The pretraining GO needs all of: material valleys in at least 25% of groups on at least 3/4 topologies; at least **8% median topology** gain from the material-valley oracle over the better exact source; and an equally informed route-aware hand selector using only **16 exact joint-plan evaluations** that captures **under 50%** of the all-pair oracle gain at the median topology. The hand selector may train on the other three topologies' exact pair labels and use local candidate state, platform counts, peer 1/2/4-hop summaries and per-core-link route loads, with leave-one-topology-out predictions. Its hyperparameters and features must be frozen before reading the complete scores. If any bar fails, do not allocate GPU. If all pass, diagnose actionable residual ranking reversals after these hand controls before separately registering matched full-bipartite, peer-only and MP-OFF training.
