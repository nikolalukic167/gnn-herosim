# l2d_rollout_headroom_v1 — future-action value before learning

> Produced on local-record-2026-10-06 at afea04bd; code not merged; old physics.

**Status:** `CLOSED` (2026-09-23) — both future-action rollout screens miss the two-size training GO. Registered 2026-09-23; the guarded portfolio follow-up was registered after the first NO-GO and evaluated on a new split.

**Outcome.** On seed 400, optimistic best-of-two future-action rollout gains 3.55% median over the four-rule hand portfolio at 6×6 and 4.07% at 10×10, below the 5% training bar at both sizes. The stronger guarded four-rule continuation on fresh seed 401 gains 6.38% at 6×6 but only 4.31% at 10×10, so the two-size bar still fails. It improves all 32 cases by construction while costing median 1.98 seconds and 26.85 seconds per instance, against 17.49 and 52.95 milliseconds for the hand portfolio. No GNN is trained on this target. This closes these complete-workflow L2D rollout mechanisms, sizes and rule controls; it does not bound all future-aware decisions or the true job-shop optimum.

**Question.** Does a complete-workflow one-step rollout offer enough makespan gain over a cheap hand-rule portfolio to justify training a future-action-value GNN? This is a new decision target after [size-specific learning efficiency](l2d_learning_efficiency_v1.md) failed. It is an L2D job-shop screen, not a HeROsim workflow result.

## Frozen screen

- Generate 16 fresh 6×6 and 16 fresh 10×10 instances from upstream L2D with NumPy seed 400. Do not select cases by outcome. Calibration used one opened seed-399 case per size and is excluded; there, the four-rule hand portfolio and the better of two rollouts scored 472/472 at 6×6 and 845/840 at 10×10.
- The hand control simulates complete schedules from SPT, MWKR, FDD/MWKR and MOPNR, then chooses the lowest makespan. Charge all four simulations. This is stronger than quoting FDD/MWKR alone.
- At every decision, each rollout policy clones the current upstream environment, forces every feasible next operation, completes the resulting schedule with one frozen continuation rule (FDD/MWKR or MWKR), and chooses the candidate with lowest final makespan, breaking ties by operation ID. Recompute after every decision. The reported *oracle* gets the better of the two complete rollout policies; charge both. This favors the future-action idea and is not a deployable learned policy.
- Replay each chosen action sequence through a fresh upstream environment and assert the same terminal makespan. Assert each rollout policy is no worse than its own continuation rule. Record all makespans, selected actions, candidate-rollout counts, dataset hash, upstream commit and measured wall times. Timing excludes the independent action replay.
- **Training GO:** at both sizes, the better-of-two rollout oracle must improve on the hand portfolio by at least **5% median paired makespan** and improve at least **12/16** instances. Report a bootstrap 95% interval over the 16 instances and full timing without using the interval to relax the GO bar. If either size fails, do not train a value model on this target. A GO would authorize only a separately registered matched GNN/MP-OFF/hand-feature training gate; it would not itself establish a GNN win.

Runner: [rollout_headroom.py](../../scripts_reeval/l2d/rollout_headroom.py). Reader: [analyze_rollout_headroom.py](../../scripts_reeval/l2d/analyze_rollout_headroom.py). Both output files and the reader result will be attached here.

## Record

- **2026-09-23 — guarded portfolio-continuation follow-up NO-GO.** The [paired read](l2d_rollout_headroom_v1/followup_read.json) covers all 16 fresh seed-401 cases per size; each complete rollout action sequence was replayed through a fresh upstream environment, and every result weakly dominates its initial hand portfolio. At 6×6, median gain is **6.38%** (bootstrap median 95% CI 2.87–7.39; 16/16 positive), so this size clears the signed bar. At 10×10, median gain is **4.31%** (2.85–6.92; 16/16), missing the signed 5% bar. The two-size GO therefore fails even for this stronger feasible reference. Median hand-portfolio versus guarded-rollout wall times are **17.49 ms versus 1,984.27 ms** at 6×6 and **52.95 ms versus 26,851.05 ms** at 10×10. The latter is about 507 times the cheap control's time; any future learner would have to keep almost all of the quality gain at a radically lower cost, then face matched timed hand search and separately trained MP-OFF. The [independent audit](../../scripts_reeval/l2d/audit_rollout_headroom.py) regenerated all four input sets and passed feasibility, makespan and hand-rule replays for all **96** saved rollout plans; [receipts](l2d_rollout_headroom_v1/audit_seed401_10x10.json) are stored beside each case file. Evidence: [6×6 cases](l2d_rollout_headroom_v1/seed401_6x6.json), [10×10 cases](l2d_rollout_headroom_v1/seed401_10x10.json), and [runner](../../scripts_reeval/l2d/portfolio_rollout_headroom.py).
- **2026-09-23 — stronger follow-up registered after the initial NO-GO, before seed-401 evaluation.** On 16 fresh seed-401 instances per size, start with the best complete action sequence of the same four hand rules. At each subsequent state, try every feasible next action and finish it under *each* of the four rules; replace the incumbent complete suffix only when a strictly lower makespan is found. This guarded receding-horizon policy cannot worsen the hand portfolio and gives the future-action idea a stronger, more expensive feasible reference. Compare it with the same four-rule portfolio, record every action and exact replay, and retain the original two-size **≥5% median and ≥12/16 positive** training GO. Because a guard can create ties, ties are not positive. Report full algorithm wall time excluding audit replay. This is a post-hoc mechanism change, so seed 401 is disjoint from the opened seed-400 cases. Runner: [portfolio_rollout_headroom.py](../../scripts_reeval/l2d/portfolio_rollout_headroom.py).
- **2026-09-23 — frozen two-continuation screen NO-GO.** The [paired read](l2d_rollout_headroom_v1/read.json) records 16 upstream-L2D cases at each size, with all 32 action sequences independently replayed per continuation. Better-of-two rollout gain over the four-rule hand portfolio is **3.55% median** (bootstrap median 95% CI 1.64–5.46; 13/16 positive) at 6×6 and **4.07%** (2.51–6.55; 14/16) at 10×10. Both consistency counts pass, but both median gains miss the signed 5% bar. Median wall times for the four-rule portfolio versus both rollouts are **17.51 versus 986.43 ms** at 6×6 and **52.64 versus 14,091.66 ms** at 10×10. The first 6×6 command computed all cases but failed while writing to an absent artifact directory; the same fixed seed was rerun after making the output path. No case was replaced. Evidence: [6×6 cases](l2d_rollout_headroom_v1/seed400_6x6.json), [10×10 cases](l2d_rollout_headroom_v1/seed400_10x10.json), and [analyzer](../../scripts_reeval/l2d/analyze_rollout_headroom.py).
- **2026-09-23 — registered.** The 16+16 fresh seed-400 cases, hand portfolio, rollout mechanisms, timing contract and two-size GO bar were fixed before generating or reading seed-400 instances.
