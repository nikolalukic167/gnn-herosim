# g32_core_prefetch_s0_v1 — core-only peer-prefetch capacity

**Status:** `CLOSED` (2026-09-23) — **NO-GO for local-move GNN training**. Registered 2026-09-23 before training-topology scoring.

**Outcome.** Core-only group-commit prefetch creates substantial, live-audited nonlocal waiting, and its bounded 128-move exact oracle improves the better baseline by 13.72% at the median topology. But a route-aware hand learner with the same 16 exact evaluations captures 72–100% of that gain on held-out topologies. The signed residual-information bar fails. No GNN was trained and no GNN win is claimed. This closes this physics plus single-reassignment/swap move pool; coordinated moves are a separate question.

**Question.** When peer state is prefetched at group commit, does capacity contention on the *multi-route core* create a material placement decision beyond endpoint counts and route-aware hand controls? [Unfiltered prefetch](g32_peer_prefetch_s0_v1.md) causes a large causal RTT penalty but fails its nonlocal bar because access-link pipes dominate. This experiment gives access links nonblocking multiplexed service while preserving their transmission holds and propagation latency; only core links retain capacity-one pipes. It is a distinct environment contract, not a retuning of the closed unfiltered result.

## 2026-09-23 — final exact residual read and fresh live gate

The finite-pipe training screen replays 64 captured groups on 9801, 9803, 9804 and 9806. Median core wait shares are **12.855%, 9.265%, 12.334%, 21.468%** (3/4 pass the signed 10% bar). Paired same-plan finite-versus-infinite RTT penalties are **24.813%, 17.553%, 29.108%, 31.202%** (4/4 pass the 8% bar). Core-only contention is measurable, with no post-read physics tuning.

The frozen deterministic pool retains 7,987 of at most 8,192 proposed moves and makes **8,111** exact baseline-plus-move evaluations across all 64 groups. Its bounded oracle gains over the better exact hand/MP-OFF plan are **9.431%, 18.003%, 18.262%, 6.985%** by topology; median **13.717%** passes the 8% oracle bar. The original cost proxy's best 16 evaluated moves capture **42.67%, 12.75%, 11.63%, 40.01%** of that gain. These are local-pool gains, never global-optimum claims.

The stronger, script-frozen route-aware hand control uses 445 scalar features: local candidate state, platform counts/load, peer 1/2/4-hop aggregates, and per-core-link route loads/holds plus a physical route proxy. Ridge and ExtraTrees hyperparameters were fixed before the full oracle read; each uses leave-one-topology-out training and the same 16 exact evaluations. Ridge captures **83.45%, 100%, 100%, 77.48%** by held-out topology; ExtraTrees captures **98.90%, 100%, 100%, 72.34%**. The residual bar fails decisively even though the causal and oracle bars pass. The full scores and predictions are in `core_prefetch_s0_inputs_2026-09-23.tar.gz` (SHA-256 `7c1192a24980e2483134296a219b7ba7953de1fc88c3307155c7e3cff1ef8b1a`) and `core_prefetch_residual_2026-09-23.tar.gz` (SHA-256 `5c6ede38313e943456562c61b36c8d44701c26d0a3975199d5e4e49c3f848b64`) beside this node. The input archive carries the isolated source patch and exact script.

The independently frozen fresh live gate ran original additive, infinite-pipe and finite-core-only modes for hand and MP-OFF on seeds **9819** and **9822**: **12 successful runs, 5,120 tasks and 160 groups each**. The audit checks task IDs 0–5119, equal plans and fixed-replica manifests across modes, 16 snapshots, no scale events, source/input hashes, nonzero peer exchange, and finite-only core wait. Finite core wait is **19.81–21.90%** of total live RTT; hand finite RTT is 5,481.777 and 5,089.645 versus infinite 4,069.361 and 3,674.404 on the two seeds. This confirms the physics survives live execution but cannot reverse the residual failure. The isolated source copy passed **17 peer tests**. Raw live evidence and audit script are archived in `core_prefetch_live_2026-09-23.tar.gz` (SHA-256 `122ed82f9affb870808f90722d1c10f447e32cdbb5b297f60d67235e1257f157`). No GPU was allocated.
